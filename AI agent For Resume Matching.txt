We are seeking an HR Operations and Talent Development Executive to manage end-to-end training coordination, recruitment support, and employee lifecycle processes. This role focuses on organizing training programs, supporting hiring initiatives, maintaining HR systems and CRM data, and ensuring smooth onboarding and performance tracking. The ideal candidate will work closely with HR leaders, trainers, and business stakeholders to deliver people-centric programs that improve workforce readiness and organizational growth.

Key Responsibilities

Plan and coordinate training batches, schedules, and resources for virtual and in-person programs

Track attendance, maintain training records, and distribute session materials and documentation

Collect and analyze training feedback and prepare effectiveness and progress reports

Support talent acquisition activities including resume screening, interview scheduling, and candidate engagement

Lead and coordinate campus hiring initiatives and recruitment drives with academic institutions

Maintain CRM and applicant tracking systems for accurate candidate and hiring data

Manage onboarding and induction processes, including documentation and orientation sessions

Assist in performance tracking and support PMS activities, appraisals, and review cycles

Prepare HR reports and dashboards using Excel and internal HR tools

Collaborate with internal teams and external stakeholders to align HR operations with staffing needs

Required Qualifications

1 to 3 years of experience in HR operations, training coordination, recruitment, or learning and development

Strong experience with resume screening, interview coordination, and candidate communication

Hands-on experience managing training programs, feedback collection, and reporting

Proficiency with CRM, ATS tools, and Excel-based tracking systems

Experience supporting onboarding and employee lifecycle processes

Strong communication, coordination, and stakeholder management skills

Preferred Qualifications

PGDM or Master’s in Human Resources or related field

Exposure to campus hiring programs and HR analytics

Familiarity with Performance Management Systems and HR documentation standards
